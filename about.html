Our base

Wuhan — the centre of it all.

Wuhan sits at the geographic heart of China, connecting north, south, east and west. It is China's brand manufacturing hub: SHEIN completes around 70% of its production in and around Wuhan. Brands like Aierfeiyang, Shengyalong, Ouliman and Maoren Group are built here.

Hanzheng Street — China's largest source wholesale market — is on our doorstep. Being here means we don't just know suppliers. We know the right ones.
