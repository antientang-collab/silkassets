Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

SilkAssets collects information you provide when making a purchase, including your name, email address, and billing information. This information is processed securely through our payment processor (Creem) and is never stored directly on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to process your order and deliver your purchased digital products. We may send you order confirmation and download emails. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Payment Processing

All payments are processed by Creem, our payment processor. SilkAssets does not store your credit card details. Please review Creem's privacy policy for information on how they handle your payment data.

4. Digital Downloads

After purchase, you will receive a download link via email. Your purchase records are stored securely to allow re-downloads if needed. Download links may expire after a set period.

5. Email List

If you sign up for our free checklist or newsletter, we collect your email address to send you the requested content and occasional updates about new products. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email.

6. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@silkassets.top.
